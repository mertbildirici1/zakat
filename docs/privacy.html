Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

The Zakat iPhone app stores your holdings, optional local profile, and history on your device. Data is treated as “collected” only when it leaves the device.

Metal-price requests do not include your holdings. If you link a bank, a linking provider and the app backend receive account names and balances for you to review. Email to support@zakat.app contains whatever you send. We do not sell data or use it for ads.

Delete a local profile in the app (Profile → Delete account) or uninstall the app. Contact support@zakat.app.
